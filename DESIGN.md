# Boneiset dashboard draft

Draft without direction: no approved brand guide yet. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

- Purpose: staff locate corporate devices, inspect reporting gaps, then correct assignments.
- Layout: numbered register and full-width data table, not decorative metric cards; device names are the focal point.
- Light background: office inventory work requires sustained reading of dense tables. Fixed light theme for this draft.
- Color: warm paper, dark ink, deep green for navigation/actions; rust for warning text, always accompanied by words.
- Type: local Franklin Gothic Medium headings and Trebuchet MS body; industrial register character without external font requests.
- Motif: a strong green register rule, compact section numbers and tabular timestamps repeated across pages.
- Motion: hover/focus only; no decorative animations in an operational interface.
- Data: database only; explicit empty states and UTC labels. No maps, images, custom icons, logos or third-party requests.
- Forms: native controls, visible labels, clear keyboard focus, server feedback, and a submission status indicator.
- Responsive: navigation wraps; wide tables scroll in named, keyboard-focusable regions; form fields stack.

Brand approval and visual assets remain outside this draft. Security and working behavior do not depend on visual approval.
